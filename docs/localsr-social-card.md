# LocalSR social-preview artwork

The site uses a bespoke typographic link-preview graphic at
[`localsr/assets/og.png`](../localsr/assets/og.png), 1738 × 905 pixels.
It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool and inspected for exact text before inclusion.
It is used as a social graphic; the site's comparison images are separate, real LocalSR output.

## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a complete landscape social link-preview graphic for LocalSR, an independent desktop image super-resolution application. Aspect ratio 1.91:1, ideally 1536x800. Exact text: small top-left wordmark 'LocalSR'; large principal headline 'Give your images' and on the next line 'a little more room.'; small bottom-left text 'Image restoration. On your computer.'; top-right small text 'ALPHA 0.0.11'. Carefully typeset, precise Swiss photographic-editorial publication, warm matte off-white background #f3f1e9, near-black olive ink #242720, one tiny vermilion #b33d26 dot near the wordmark, pale sage #e0e5d7. Headline first line in a clean sturdy grotesque similar to DM Sans; second line graceful italic serif similar to Newsreader. Very generous negative space and exact baseline alignment. Thin horizontal ink rules at top and bottom. An abstract photographic crop-registration bracket motif in the far right corner, using simple thin straight lines, suggests enlargement. No photography, no AI imagery, no gradients, no glowing objects, no 3D, no rounded UI cards, no stars, no logos from other companies, no extra words, no invented capabilities. This is a finished flat typographic social graphic, not a photographed mockup, and not a screenshot of the website.
