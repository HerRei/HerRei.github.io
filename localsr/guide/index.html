User guide · LocalSR beta for macOS · 17 September 2026

From first photo
to finished result.

Everything you need to get good results from LocalSR, in the order you will need it. Reading the first three sections takes about five minutes.

Install

  1. You need a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. More memory lets you use the larger models and bigger outputs.
  2. Download the DMG from the download page, open it and drag LocalSR into Applications.
  3. Open LocalSR from Applications. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS only asks you to confirm that you want to open something downloaded from the internet.

On the first launch a short welcome appears. It links back to this guide and mentions the anonymous update-check count, which you can turn off right there.

The window

  • Media on the left: everything you added, with its status. Switch between Single and Batch at the top.
  • The preview in the middle: drop files here. After processing it shows the before-and-after comparison.
  • Enhance on the right: what to do (the task), how (quality, content, what to fix first) and, under Advanced, output and hardware settings.
  • The status bar at the bottom: progress, time estimates, the start button, and Reveal and Open for the selected item’s result.
  • The toolbar at the top: Model library, Performance and the … menu (updates, the user guide, diagnostics, system integrations).

In a narrow window the three panels become tabs: Media, Preview and Enhance.

Your first image

  1. Add a photo. Drop it onto the preview, or click the preview to choose files. JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF and DNG camera RAW work.
  2. Choose a task under Enhance: Upscale makes photos and artwork 2×, 3× or 4× larger; Restore removes noise, blur or JPEG artifacts without enlarging; Upscale Video is for clips.
  3. Choose the quality. Quick is fast and good for most photos. Best is slower and keeps finer texture. Models are not included in the installer: the first time, press Get Quick (a few megabytes) or Quick + Best. They download once, are checked, and stay on your Mac.
  4. Say what the image is. Pick Photo or Illustration, and under Fix first choose problems to clean up before enlarging, such as noise, JPEG artifacts or blur. The plan card underneath shows which model runs at each step, its license, its size and whether it fits your hardware.
  5. Start with the blue button at the bottom right. It says Upscale selected or Restore selected, depending on the task.
  6. Check the result in the comparison, then press Open to view the file or Reveal to show it in Finder.

Upscaling models invent plausible detail. Text, faces, eyes and fine patterns can look convincing and still be wrong, so look closely before you rely on a result. LocalSR never overwrites your original.

Where results go

Results are always saved as new files. By default they go to your Desktop; change the folder under Advanced → Save to. File → Open Output Folder opens it.

The name tells you what happened: photo_upscaled_4x.png, scan_denoised.png or clip_video_2x.mp4. If a file with that name exists, a number is added instead of replacing it.

Open and Reveal always act on the item selected in the Media list. Select a different finished item to open its result; the buttons are hidden while the selected item has no result yet.

Compare before and after

When the selected item has a result, the preview shows the original and the enhanced version side by side. Drag the divider, or focus it and use the arrow keys. View → Actual Pixels (1:1) shows real pixels, which is the honest way to judge detail; View → Fit to Window zooms back out. For videos, both versions play in sync.

Many files at once

  1. Switch the Media list to Batch, or use Add Folder…: a folder becomes a batch.
  2. Choose the task and quality once. The start button now says Start 12 items (or however many there are).
  3. While the queue runs, the status bar shows an estimate for the current item and the whole queue, measured from items already finished. You can keep adding files with Add selected to queue, or stop with Cancel queue.

Images and videos need separate runs, because they use different tasks. Try one representative file first before processing a large folder.

Models, presets and recipes

Model library in the toolbar lists every model with what it is good at, its size, author, license and source. Download or remove models there, or pin a model to the Quick or Best preset for a task. The model guide explains each model in plain words.

If you often use the same setup, press Save current setup as recipe. Recipes appear next to Quick and Best and in the Presets menu.

You can also use your own model: in the library, under Your own file, choose a checkpoint. .safetensors files are strongly recommended. The two optional HAT face models are only available as copies you supply yourself, because their rights are unresolved.

Video

  • Choose Upscale Video, add a clip and press Start selected video. Every frame goes through the image model, and the original timing, rotation and audio are kept.
  • iPhone and camera videos (H.264, HEVC, AAC) open directly on a Mac. WMV, DivX, FLV and a few older formats need FFmpeg; LocalSR shows the install command when a file needs it and uses an installed FFmpeg automatically.
  • Exports use AV1 by default. Under Advanced → Video format you can choose VP9, lossless FFV1, or H.264 and HEVC. macOS writes H.264 and HEVC with its own encoders into MP4, and those files play almost everywhere.
  • Video is slow on any computer. Minutes of footage can take hours. Start with a few seconds, check the result, then run the whole clip.
  • HDR preservation, de-flicker and the SeedVR2 video models are marked Labs: they work, but their quality is not established yet.

Output and hardware settings

Open Advanced at the bottom of the Enhance panel.

  • Output scale and Format (PNG, JPEG, TIFF or WebP) for images; Save to for the output folder.
  • Preserve safe metadata and color profile converts colors to sRGB with a matching profile and copies only the date, artist and copyright. Location, camera serial numbers and similar details are never copied.
  • Hardware chooses the Apple GPU (Metal) or the CPU. The GPU is usually much faster.
  • Tile, Halo and Precision control how large images are split to fit in memory. Leave them alone unless you run out of memory; Safe memory mode is the simple switch for that.
  • Interface text makes the whole window larger.

Updates and privacy

LocalSR checks for a newer beta each time it starts. When one is available, a notice offers Update Now: the update is downloaded, verified against LocalSR’s signing key, installed, and the app restarts. It waits until a running queue has finished. Settings, recipes and models are kept. … → Check for Updates… checks by hand.

Your photos and videos never leave your Mac. The update check sends an anonymous count (app version, platform and release channel, no identifiers). Turn it off in the welcome notice or in Software Update. The privacy page describes every connection the app makes.

Finder and the command line

… → System Integrations… installs an Enhance with LocalSR Quick Action for Finder, so you can right-click photos, videos or folders and send them to LocalSR with the Quick or Best preset. It also adds a localsr-next command for scripts. Move LocalSR to Applications first; both can be removed from the same dialog.

Troubleshooting

  • Open or Reveal is missing. Select an item in the Media list that has finished. The buttons belong to the selected item.
  • It is slow. Check that Hardware is the Apple GPU, try Quick, use a smaller output scale, and close other heavy apps. Performance runs a short benchmark to compare the GPU and CPU on your Mac.
  • It stopped with a memory error. Turn on Safe memory mode or choose a smaller model. Very large images and 4× video need a lot of memory.
  • A video will not open. The format probably needs FFmpeg; follow the install command LocalSR shows. If it still fails, report the format.
  • The result looks plastic or wrong. Try another model or Photo instead of Illustration, and turn off fixes you do not need. Some damage cannot be recovered.
  • The app says the model does not fit. The plan card compares the model with your free memory; pick a smaller model or close other apps.

Still stuck? … → Copy Diagnostics copies a hardware and app summary without file names or media. Paste it into a report on GitHub Issues, or email it; the support page says what else helps.
