What it does

Every model runs on your computer. Each one shows its size, licence and source before it downloads.

Upscale

Enlarge photos and artwork by 2×, 3× or 4×. Quick is a good start, and Best spends more time on photo detail. Which model for which photo

Restore

Clean up noise, blur and JPEG artifacts first, then enlarge. Or keep the size and only repair. LocalSR vs Upscayl

Video Labs

Enhance video frame by frame, with the source timing, rotation and audio kept. Fine detail can flicker, and long clips take hours. About video

Edit with a prompt Experimental, Mac only

Describe the change, and FLUX.2 klein or Qwen Image Edit makes a new image. The original stays as it was. About editing

Folders and formats

Process a whole folder or a queue, and save a setup as a recipe. Opens JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF, AVIF and camera RAW, and HEIC on a Mac.

Your own models

Load a model file you trust, for example from OpenModelDB, in the model library. All built-in models

Why I built it

LocalSR started out of necessity. For a long time I upscaled pictures with the HAT-L models, but only through the terminal. At some point I wanted a proper app for it, and most of what I found was subscription-based, really complicated or outdated. So the decision was clear.

Hermes Reisner

Limits

  • Upscaling reconstructs detail. Faces, text and fine patterns can look sharp and still differ from the real scene, especially at 4×.
  • Video is upscaled one frame at a time, so fine detail can flicker.
  • Large images and long videos can take hours. Try a small one first.
  • The Mac build is tested the most.

Results are saved as new files, so your originals stay untouched.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. LocalSR is open source under the MIT license, with nothing to buy or sign up for. The models you download keep their own licenses; the model guide lists them.

Will it use my graphics card?

On a Mac, yes: the Neural Engine and the Apple Silicon GPU, with a CPU fallback. On Windows and Linux the standard download runs on the CPU. GPU editions exist for AMD (ROCm on Linux, tested on a Radeon RX 9060 XT), NVIDIA (CUDA on Windows and Linux) and any DirectX 12 card (DirectML on Windows).

What does “beta” mean here?

Features can still change. The Mac build is signed, notarized and tells you when a newer beta is available. The Windows and Linux packages pass the same automated checks but have not had the manual testing the Mac build gets, and they do not update themselves yet. Keep your originals.

Can it recover the original detail?

Super-resolution estimates plausible detail from an image. It cannot guarantee the recovery of information that was lost, and results vary by source and model. Inspect small text, faces and fine patterns carefully.

Are the models included?

Most models download separately, when you first choose them. LocalSR checks every download against its expected size and SHA-256 checksum.
