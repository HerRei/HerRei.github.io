Two HAT face-restoration experiments, blended back toward their stock models to balance damaged-face recovery with clean-image fidelity.

AvailabilityHAT-S FaceHAT-L FaceUse in LocalSRLimits & terms

Prepared for a later release

The complete HAT-S and HAT-L companion integration is prepared separately from the ongoing v0.0.12-alpha release. This page does not announce a new installer. Stock HAT remains the general-purpose choice within each pair.

Both face checkpoints are optional Labs models, supplied by the user and verified locally. They are excluded from automatic downloads because checkpoint and training-data rights remain unresolved. HAT-L Face has no public weight download.

HAT-S Face · the lighter model

The selected blend combines 90% stock HAT-S with 10% of the face-tuned 95k checkpoint (α = 0.10). Interpolation recovered a useful model after the original GAN/perceptual fine-tune regressed on clean images.

HAT-L Face · the larger model

Three L1-only stages ran for 250k, 250k, and 299k iterations, without GAN or perceptual loss. Training plateaued in a roughly 0.1 dB band. Raw face-tuned weights improved mildly degraded faces by about 1 dB but lost about 1.9 dB on clean inputs.

From 14 candidates, the selected blend combines 75% stock ImageNet HAT-L and 25% face-tuned run 3 (α = 0.25). Its reported results balance recovery with a much smaller clean-image cost.

HAT-L model card and selection notes

Use the matching companion

  1. In a build containing this integration, select stock HAT-S or stock HAT-L as the primary checkpoint.
  2. Enable its face-aware pass. A supported local OpenCV face detector must be available.
  3. Acknowledge the checkpoint terms and choose an exact external copy you are permitted to use. LocalSR checks the file's size and SHA-256.
  4. Adjust face fidelity and inspect the result. Non-face regions retain the primary model's output.

Tauri exposes face models as companions. The legacy Slint frontend also supports direct face-model selection. This integration does not bundle either checkpoint.

Measured gains, with limits

These reports use synthetic degradation and checkpoint-selection sets. They do not establish a controlled HAT-S versus HAT-L comparison, general photographic improvements, or video quality. The larger model costs more memory and processing time.

Neither interpolation nor L1-only training guarantees accurate missing detail or preserved identity. Results can look smooth or contain incorrect texture. Keep the original image and inspect eyes, teeth, hair, and other fine features.

Apache-2.0 applies to the HAT source code; it does not independently establish rights to fine-tuned weights or their training images. No commercial-use claim is made. The existing HAT-S release is provenance evidence, not rights clearance; HAT-L weights remain unpublished.
