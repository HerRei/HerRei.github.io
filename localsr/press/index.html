In a few sentences

LocalSR is a free desktop app for upscaling photos, cleaning up noise and blur, and enhancing video. Choose an image, pick Quick or Best, and compare the result with your original. The models run on your computer; your photos stay there too.

On Apple Silicon Macs, Quick runs on the Neural Engine and Best through Core ML on the GPU, two to three times as fast as before. LocalSR is open source and made by one person alongside uni.

Removing it

On a Mac, quit LocalSR and move it to the Trash. Then delete ~/Library/Application Support/LocalSR, which holds the models, settings and queue, and ~/Library/Caches/LocalSR. If you turned on the Finder action, also delete ~/Library/Services/Enhance with LocalSR.workflow. Exported files stay where you saved them.
