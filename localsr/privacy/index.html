Local processing

LocalSR processes the images and videos you choose on your computer. It does not upload them for enhancement or use them to train models. No LocalSR account, advertising, app usage analytics or automatic diagnostic reporting is included.

The app reads your files and metadata, creates previews and writes separate results. The local queue stores file paths, thumbnails, media properties, settings, job status and output paths. Recipes, benchmarks, models, temporary files and recovery backups are also local. Closing the app does not erase them.

Network connections

Model downloads contact the source shown in the catalog, such as GitHub or Hugging Face. The service receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and the requested file. Source media is not included in download requests.

The .13 macOS alpha has no active automatic-update feed. Future direct editions may contact a configured release service when checking or downloading updates. Planned Store editions will use Microsoft Store for application updates. External website and license links open services with their own privacy policies.

Installer and source downloads are served from the maintainer’s Mac mini through Tailscale Funnel. These services process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses and requested paths. The Caddy download service has access logging disabled. Tailscale’s own handling is described in its privacy policy.

Sharing is your choice

Copy diagnostics puts a hardware and app summary on your clipboard when requested. It omits media names, paths and content. Benchmark reports can be saved locally. Neither is automatically sent. Review logs, screenshots and pasted reports before sharing; they can contain information you add or error details. Clipboard synchronization, operating-system backups and crash reporting follow your system settings.

Storage and removal

Direct editions use ~/Library/Application Support/LocalSR on macOS, %LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalSR on Windows, and ${XDG_DATA_HOME:-~/.local/share}/LocalSR on Linux. Windows Store data can also be virtualized beneath its package's LocalCache folder. Settings and recipes live under next; model checkpoints live under models. Exports use the chosen output folder.

Removing a queue item does not delete its original or completed exports. Backups can retain older queue information. For complete removal, quit LocalSR, keep a backup of anything needed and delete the relevant local data and exports. This also removes settings, recipes and models. The tested Windows Store uninstall deleted its package profile. Back up that profile before uninstalling if you want to retain it. A verified backup restored it after reinstallation. Other packaging routes may leave local data behind.

Support and website

Public GitHub issue reports, usernames and attachments are visible to others. For private contact and privacy requests, email hermes.reisner@gmail.com. Email is received in the project's Gmail inbox and is not automatically posted to GitHub. Do not send credentials or signing keys.

The website uses GitHub Pages and locally hosted assets, with no added analytics script. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement and Pages visitor information.

The publisher is Hermes Reisner, with HerRei as project branding. Contact the address above to request removal of information you sent for support.
