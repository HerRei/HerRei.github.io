License

LocalSR’s own code is free software under the MIT license. You can use, copy, change and share it, also commercially, as long as the copyright and license notice stay with it. There is no account and nothing to pay.

The installers also contain libraries under their own licenses, listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. Some of them, like FFmpeg under the LGPL, need their source to go along when you share an installer. Each release has that source next to it.

Models

No model is bundled. Each model downloads from its author when you choose it and keeps its own license, which the app shows before the download. You are responsible for following it.

Your files

LocalSR runs on your computer, so I never see what you process. You need the right to use the images and videos you open, and you are responsible for what you make with them, including pictures created with Edit. Please don’t use it to deceive people with edited pictures of real people.

No warranty

LocalSR is a beta made by one person alongside uni. It comes as it is, without any warranty, as the MIT license says. AI results can be wrong, and a job can fail or take much longer than expected. Keep your originals. As far as Swiss law allows, I am not liable for damage caused by using LocalSR.
